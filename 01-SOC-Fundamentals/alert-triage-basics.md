# 🚨 Alert Triage Basics

Alert triage is the process of reviewing and analyzing alerts to determine their importance and risk.

---

## 🔍 What is an Alert?

An alert is generated by security tools (SIEM, EDR, IDS) when suspicious activity matches a rule or pattern.

Alert ≠ Attack

---

## 🧠 SOC Triage Process

1. **Observe**
   - What happened?
   - Which system?
   - Which user?

2. **Analyze**
   - Is this normal behavior?
   - Any suspicious patterns?

3. **Decide**
   - False Positive (normal)
   - True Positive (real threat)

4. **Act**
   - Close
   - Escalate

---

## ⚖ Severity vs Priority

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
Severity | Technical risk assigned by tool |
Priority | Business impact decided by analyst |

---

## 🔑 Principle

> “Do not trust the alert blindly — trust the investigation.”
