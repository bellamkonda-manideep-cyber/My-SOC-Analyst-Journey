# 📝 Incident Documentation Principles

SOC analysts must document every investigation clearly.

---

## 📌 Why Documentation Matters

- Helps SOC L2/L3 continue investigation
- Required for audits
- Supports incident response
- Shows analyst reasoning

---

## 🧠 Good SOC Notes Include

- What happened
- When it happened
- Affected system
- Evidence reviewed
- Decision made
- Reason for decision

---

## ✍ Example Note

“Observed 5 failed login attempts followed by a successful login and sudo action. Pattern suggests possible credential misuse. Escalating for further investigation.”

---

## 🔑 Principle

> “If it’s not documented, it didn’t happen.”
