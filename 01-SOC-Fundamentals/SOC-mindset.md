# 🧠 SOC Analyst Mindset

A Security Operations Center (SOC) Analyst is not just a person who looks at alerts — they are defenders responsible for protecting an organization from security threats.

---

## 🎯 Role of a SOC Analyst

SOC Analysts are the **first line of defense**. Their responsibilities include:

- Monitoring security alerts
- Investigating suspicious activity
- Determining if events are real threats
- Escalating confirmed incidents
- Documenting findings clearly

---

## 🧩 Key Mindset Principles

### 1. Evidence Over Assumption
Never assume an alert is malicious or safe without checking logs and context.

### 2. Context Over Severity
A high-severity alert may be harmless if context shows normal activity. A low-severity alert may be dangerous if it impacts critical systems.

### 3. Business Impact Matters
SOC decisions are based on how an incident affects:
- Business operations
- Sensitive data
- Critical systems

### 4. Stay Calm Under Pressure
SOC environments can be noisy. Analysts must stay calm and analytical.

### 5. Think in Attack Stages
Instead of viewing isolated alerts, think in stages:
Execution → Persistence → Lateral Movement → Impact → Exfiltration

---

## 🔑 Core SOC Principle

> “An alert is just a signal. Investigation determines the truth.”
